### Problem/Challenge
Modern society heavily depends on IT systems for daily infrastructure and essential services. At the same time, the number of cyber attacks and security weakness is increasing rapidly. Therefore, a Security Operations Center (SOC) has become essential for organizations. As the core of an organization’s defense system, SOC monitors business services and IT systems continuously. It helps identify security weaknesses, detect policy violations, and quickly respond to cyber incidents.
 However, there are a lot of challenges to build an effective SOC. For example, Tier 1 analysts need to check huge amount alerts everyday and almost of them are false positives. This is leading to serious threats being left unaddressed and analysts becoming burned out. In addition, SOC have problems such as finding and keeping staff who have skills, standardizing processes, integrating technology, and managing compliance. To solve these problems, whole organizations must work together, and the cybersecurity industry needs to share best practices and standard rules.
This exercise focused on understanding the organizational structure in defensive security. Especially, I learned the clear boundaries and responsibilities of each role including Red/GRC/Blue team, SOC, and CIRT. Also simulating the proper operational approach (role-based incident escalation) regarding who should respond to various incidents and how primary objective in this exercise was.
### Methodology
Task 2: Security Hierarchy
I studied how security departments are structured in company. I learned that the Chief Information Security Officer(CISO）leads the security team based on business needs. 

Security Departments

・Red team: Offensive security 

・Blue team: Defensive security

・GRC team: Managing policies and ensuring compliance 

Task 3: Meet the Blue Team
I learned the internal roles within the Blue Team and the Security Operations Center (SOC), Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT), and Specialized Defensive Roles (Digital Forensics Analyst, Threat Intelligence Analyst).
Security Operations Center (SOC)

・L1 Analysts: Junior members who triage alerts and pass complex cases to L2

・L2 Analysts: Experienced members who investigate more advanced attacks

・Engineers: Experts in configuring security tools like EDR or SIEM

・Manager: A person who manages the whole SOC team

Task４: Advancing SOC Career
I compared Managed Security Service Providers (MSSP) with Internal (In-house) SOC teams and learned about career pathways for a Tier 1 Analyst.

Task 5: Final Challenge 
In final task, I investigated how companies run their security operations. I tried to select the appropriate person to deal with as many as seven incidents that are happening at the same time as a CISO of TrySecureMe. To complete this task, I need to have a clear understanding of the role of security.

Triage alert created by SIEM: Tier 1 SOC Analyst

Deep analysis phishing malware: Tier 2 SOC Analyst

Response ransomware: Digital Forensics Analyst

Help PCI DSS audit: GRC Team

Check new version tryhackme vulnerabilities: Red Team

Investigate the SIME issue due to storage limit: SOC Engineer

Analyze threat group activity: CIRT
