## project objectives
In this project, I learned the organizational cyber security architecture through practical exercises in the TryHackMe room, “SOC Role in Blue team”. To complete this activity, I had to completely understand security management and the key security roles. Through this exercise, I learned that the real-world incident response workflows and proper role assignment and monitoring improve the overall defensive security skill of the organizations.

### Task 3 Meet the Blue Team
I learned the internal roles within the Blue Team and the Security Operations Center (SOC), Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT), and Specialized Defensive Roles (Digital Forensics Analyst, Threat Intelligence Analyst).

Security Operations Center (SOC)

・L1 Analysts: Junior members who triage alerts and pass complex cases to L2

・L2 Analysts: Experienced members who investigate more advanced attacks

・Engineers: Experts in configuring security tools like EDR or SIEM

・Manager: A person who manages the whole SOC team

### Task 5  Final Challenge 
In final task, I investigated how companies run their security operations. I tried to select the appropriate person to deal with as many as seven incidents that are happening at the same time as a CISO of TrySecureMe. To complete this task, I need to have a clear understanding of the role of security.

Triage alert created by SIEM: Tier 1 SOC Analyst

Deep analysis phishing malware: Tier 2 SOC Analyst

Response ransomware: Digital Forensics Analyst

Help PCI DSS audit: GRC Team

Check new version tryhackme vulnerabilities: Red Team

Investigate the SIME issue due to storage limit: SOC Engineer
Analyze threat group activity: CIRT
